Privacy Policy

Shopa Orders helps small businesses track orders. We store: your account email, business name and WhatsApp number; and the customer names, phone numbers and orders you enter. Data is stored securely with Supabase and is only visible to the account that created it. We do not sell data or use advertising trackers.

You are responsible for the customer information you enter. You can delete your account and all data at any time in the app (Plan tab > Delete my account), or by emailing YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.

Payments are processed by Paystack; we never see your card details.
